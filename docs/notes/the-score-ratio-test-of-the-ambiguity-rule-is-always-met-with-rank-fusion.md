The ratio test of the ambiguity rule must not be taken on the rank-fused score: it is almost always met, so the coverage test alone decided. D-38 takes it on the pre-fusion score.

Evidence (WP-20, 2026-11-18, MiniLM, committed weights): the fused scores of the first four metric hits of a golden question differ by 1 to 3 percent (`0.01844`, `0.01815`, `0.01780`, ...), because a rank 1 and a rank 2 of one list differ by (60+1)/(60+2). Rank 1 against rank 10 of a list is still 0.87. The rule of `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` 8.4 (score ratio 0.85 and coverage gap 0.15) therefore reduces to "the coverage of the two candidates differs by less than 0.15".

What follows:

- A question whose words are in the names of two metrics equally is ambiguous, whichever name is longer. "Outstanding principal" is in `outstanding_principal` and in `restructured_outstanding_principal`; "disburse" is in `disbursement_amount` and `disbursed_contract_count`. On the golden set this makes 12 of 36 `answer` questions `ambiguous` (`docs/EVALS.md` section 6).
- A signal for the tighter name (the share of the metric's own name that the question uses) separates those, but it also separates "Berapa NPF kita?" (G16), which must stay ambiguous between the ratio and the amount. One number cannot do both; do not add a tie-break to fix the count of one question. Changing the rule is the owner's decision.
- **Resolved by D-38 (owner, 2026-11-18):** the ratio is taken on `w_keyword * keyword_score + w_vector * vector_similarity` (`Hit.pre_fusion_score`), which fusion does not compress. Measured on the golden questions: 13 of 15 in G01-G20 (was 9), 29 of 36 (was 19), G16 and G17 still ambiguous, all 6 clarify questions still ask. The cosine alone (24 of 36) and an exact-name rule (20 of 36) did less; a name-precision signal broke G16. The figures are from the golden questions only: recheck them on paraphrased questions.

The two constants and the one function are in `makna.search.confidence` (`AMBIGUITY_SCORE_RATIO`, `AMBIGUITY_COVERAGE_GAP`, `is_ambiguous`), used by the resolver and the search service. A question's "kredit macet" is no longer ambiguous between the two NPF metrics after D-38.

Related, from the review of WP-20: the qualifier words added to `makna.search.terms` (ordinals, "final", "sepanjang", "throughout", "over") changed the coverage of 12 of 97 golden and held-out questions and the search `confidence` band of 9 of them (8 medium to high, 1 low to medium) with hit ids and scores identical, and no gate sees a band change. When the vocabulary changes, compare the bands of the golden questions before and after, not only the gated numbers. Disclosed in ARCHITECTURE 8.3; pinned by `tests/integration/test_search_qualifier_words.py`. The D-38 ratio also depends on the scale of the keyword score: re-measure after a change of keyword scoring, the weights, or the model.
